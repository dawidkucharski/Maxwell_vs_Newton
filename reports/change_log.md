## Claims removed or downgraded

1. **Mode counts 254,542 / 97,301 (removed).** Not reproducible from any committed script or JSON. Replaced by reproducible counts 127,472 / 61,631 (bins with per-mode SNR > 1, difference-field noise floor, N=512) generated by new `verification/audit_mode_counts.py`.
2. **"Scaling law … predict, before measurement" (downgraded).** Independent audit (`verification/audit_scaling_law.py`): slope 1.904±0.134 reproduces; blocked CV R²=0.518, leave-tip 0.523, leave-NA 0.482, null R²=0 — the power-law direction survives; but median prediction error in the RMSE ratio is ≈factor 2 (p90 ≈factor 13) and residuals correlate with roughness (r=0.81). The manuscript now presents the relation as an *order-of-magnitude descriptor*, the section is retitled "Empirical Scaling Relations", and the abstract says "approximately described". The example's predictions are labelled order-of-magnitude.
3. **Permanent groove depth ≈10 nm and effective-radius formula (removed, SI §1.1).** These extrapolated the *elastic* Hertzian radius into the plastic regime (p_max > H). Replaced by an explicit statement that the elastic analysis is not quantitatively valid there and that an elastic–plastic model is out of scope.
4. **"Coherence envelope ≡ lateral low-pass filter" (removed, SI §1.2).** The envelope sets axial sectioning; steep-slope truncation is a nonlinear data-loss mechanism, not a linear lateral transfer.
5. **"Skin depth ⇒ WLI probes top 15 nm" (removed, SI §1.4).** Reworded: skin depth bounds the probed material volume; WLI height comes from reflected phase; the contrast with the stylus contact depth is illustrative.
6. **"Fusion is the optimal strategy" (downgraded, main + SI).** Now: minimum-variance linear fusion under the stated independent-noise linear observation model; experimental weights require calibrated H and noise estimates; the demo uses known synthetic quantities.
7. **"No algorithm can beat MMSE" (qualified, main).** The statement is now explicitly scoped to the stated linear Gaussian-channel model; for the nonlinear dilation channel it is a linearised benchmark.
8. **"Instrument-limited metric (CV=0.22)" (qualified, abstract + Conclusions).** CV=0.16 for the random rough classes (Gaussian+ground), 0.22 with the saturating deterministic classes, 0.067 in the six-class test — stated like-for-like.
9. **Confocal/focus-variation "carries over without modification" (qualified, Limitations).** Now "expected to carry over; a modality-specific derivation … is required".
10. **Public-data benchmark scope (qualified, Results).** Explicitly a model-description check (fitted: background amplitude, slope, cutoff scale c), not an independent OTF measurement.
11. **"Scaling law" terminology** in the worked example and abstract replaced by "empirical relation/power law"; sweep-fit convention (f_c,opt = NA/λ) and single-realisation design (seed 42) now disclosed.
